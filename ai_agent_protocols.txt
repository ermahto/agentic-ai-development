AI Agent Protocols

This repository offers a comprehensive exploration of the protocols, frameworks, and architectural patterns behind modern, interconnected AI agents. It focuses on how individual agents, tools, models, and services can communicate, coordinate, and collaborate to solve complex problems efficiently. Through practical examples and implementations, the repository demonstrates how different technologies can be combined to build reliable, scalable, and intelligent multi-agent systems. It covers key concepts such as agent communication, orchestration, tool integration, context sharing, interoperability, and workflow management. The goal is to provide developers and researchers with a practical foundation for understanding how autonomous AI systems can work together rather than operate as isolated components. Whether you are experimenting with agent-based architectures, building production-ready AI applications, or exploring emerging standards for agent interoperability, this repository serves as a hands-on reference for designing, implementing, and understanding sophisticated multi-agent ecosystems and the technologies that enable them.
